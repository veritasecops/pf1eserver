## Create your account

On the login page select **Create a Player account**. Enter your display name, a unique email address, and a password of 12–128 characters. Confirm the password and select **Create Player account**. When email is configured, check the welcome email and confirm your address before opening the workspace. If email is unavailable, sign-up retains the previous immediate-access behavior.

Every public sign-up creates an active Player account, including the first account on a new instance. Only an Admin can grant Game Master or Admin access. Public registration cannot choose a privileged role.

## Email and passwords

Emails are normalized to lowercase and must be unique on the instance. A welcome/verification email confirms mailbox ownership when delivery is configured. The one-use link expires after 24 hours. Use your own email and keep campaign invitation links private. See [account emails](/help/account-email).

Passwords are stored as Argon2 hashes. Your chosen password is not temporary. You can change it in **Account security** and enroll an authenticator using the [two-factor guide](/help/two-factor).

If an account already uses your email, sign in or select **Forgot your password?** to request a reset email. Sign-up permits ten submissions per IP address per fifteen minutes; wait if the limit is reached.

## Join an invited campaign

Open your invitation link, then select **Create a Player account** on the login page. Register using the email named in the invitation. After sign-up you return to the invitation and select **Accept invitation**. Creating an account alone does not join a campaign.

## Start playing

Use **Characters** to create your own characters. Use **My profile** to edit your display information. Your overview lists your characters and joined campaigns. Ask an Admin for Game Master access if you will run campaigns.
