## Create and share a link

Open a campaign you own as a Game Master, or any campaign as an Admin. Under the invitation panel, enter the intended user's account email and select **Create invitation**.

The next page shows a personal link. Copy it and share it through your normal communication channel. Campaign invitations are not emailed automatically. Only a hash of the token is stored, so the full readable link is shown once. Create a replacement if you lose it.

Each invitation expires after seven days and can be accepted once. The signed-in account must match the normalized email entered by the inviter. Mailbox ownership is not verified, so keep invitation links private.

## Accept an invitation

Open the shared link and sign in with the invited account. If needed, finish your temporary-password change and required TOTP enrollment first. Confirm the campaign invitation with **Accept invitation**.

If you have no account, select **Create a Player account** on the login page and register with the invited email. The invitation is preserved through sign-up. An invitation grants membership, not a global role or automatic GM authority.

Joining does not move all your characters into the campaign. Assign individual characters through their edit form. Those assigned characters become manageable by the owning GM; your standalone and unrelated-campaign characters remain outside that GM's access.

## Revoke or replace

The campaign's invitation list shows pending, accepted, revoked, and expired status. The owner or an Admin can revoke a pending invitation. Revocation prevents future acceptance but does not remove an already joined member.

To end existing membership, use **Remove** beside the joined member. Their characters assigned to that campaign become unassigned and remain owned by them. The GM then loses campaign-based access. The campaign owner cannot be removed as a member; an Admin can transfer ownership instead.

## If the link fails

Expired, revoked, and used links return a not-found message. A wrong signed-in email returns an access error. Sign out and use the invited email, or ask the GM for a new invitation. If your account email changed, request a new link for the current email.
